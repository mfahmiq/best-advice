Competition: `u-pangg7-the-best-advice-i-ever-got-358cefb0` ($2, deadline 2026-11-26T20:05Z).
Task: one sentence, max 200 chars, in English, about the best advice the writer ever received.

Judge brief (criteria in weight order):
1. Single sentence about the best advice the writer ever received (off-subject loses to any on-subject sentence).
2. The advice is worth carrying: specific, earned, true beyond the moment.
3. Sounds like a real voice recalling a real moment, not a slogan or fortune cookie.
4. Tight: every word earns its place, reads in one breath.
5. It lands, leaving the reader with something to keep after the last word.
A wise-sounding line with no person behind it reads as filler. Cleverness for its own sake does not count.
